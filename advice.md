**What's working:** Share rate (0.34%) is your strongest metric, so people are sending this teaser on.

**Weakest metric:** Completion rate (24%). Viewers are leaving before the chorus pays off the hook.

2. **End on the chorus and loop back to the hook.** Finish right after the chorus lands, then return to "Wait for the chorus" in the last frame so the ending closes the hook. Put the pre-save prompt after the payoff, not before it, and test whether completion improves.
